\begin{abstract}
Implicit (latent) reasoning is often judged by task accuracy, yet high accuracy can arise from off-manifold shortcuts that ignore semantic dynamics.
We propose \textbf{SMIR} (Semantic Manifold Implicit Reasoning): a geometric certificate that a model implements authentic implicit reasoning if and only if its latent trajectory stays in a tubular neighborhood of a semantics-induced manifold, its latent vector field is conjugate to the semantic dynamics, and its readout is intervention-faithful on that chart.
We prove a \emph{certificate decomposition} into chart~$E$, dynamics~$V$, and readout~$Q$; show that \emph{wrong charts are uncertifiable}; and give a gated learnability condition that separates a Hard-$V$ feasibility upper bound from learnable routers.
On a controlled register-shadow benchmark, a Hard chart+dynamics baseline reaches Oracle-F~$0.59$ and OOD~$0.87$; gated Discrete and Soft routers transfer the readout and attain Flow-F~$0.55$--$0.57$ with OOD~$\ge 0.85$, while a text last-token chart yields F~$\approx 0$ with tubular collapse---matching the wrong-chart theorem.
Ablations show that unstructured task-only dynamics destroy conjugacy, and that Oracle-F alone is insufficient when evaluating learnable flows.
SMIR reframes latent CoT evaluation as a falsifiable geometric protocol rather than accuracy alone.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Large language models increasingly ``think'' in continuous latent states rather than (or in addition to) explicit token traces~\citep{hao2024coconut,zhu2025codi}.
This shift promises denser computation, but it also makes evaluation harder: the final-answer accuracy of a latent reasoner does \emph{not} certify that intermediate updates track semantic computation.
Models can answer correctly while remaining insensitive to interventions on the states that supposedly carry reasoning~\citep{turpin2023faithfulness,lanham2023measuring}, and recent analyses of latent CoT show that activation-level causal dependence can decay along training~\citep{faithful2025latent}.

A concrete failure mode motivates our work.
Suppose a shadow task provides ground-truth intermediate machine states $s_t$ and a distinguishing intervention that flips the label when $s_T$ is replaced by a wrong but nearby state $s_T^-$.
A model that merely memorizes surface cues may keep high task accuracy yet assign nearly identical readouts to $E(s_T)$ and $E(s_T^-)$.
Worse, if the encoder $E$ itself folds $s_T$ and $s_T^-$ together (a \emph{wrong chart}), \emph{no} downstream readout can recover intervention faithfulness.
Accuracy-centric reporting cannot distinguish these cases from genuine latent reasoning.

Causal abstraction and distributed alignment search~\citep{geiger2021causal,geiger2024das} test whether neural subspaces implement symbolic variables, and on-manifold intervention work argues that off-manifold patches are ill-posed~\citep{onmanifold2025}.
These lines motivate geometry and interventions, yet they do not provide an operational certificate that jointly constrains (i)~the representation chart, (ii)~multi-step latent dynamics, and (iii)~readout faithfulness, together with a theorem explaining when popular interfaces (e.g., last-token text embeddings) are \emph{structurally} uncertifiable.

We introduce \textbf{SMIR} (Semantic Manifold Implicit Reasoning).
Informally, latent reasoning is authentic when the model's internal trajectory stays near a semantics-induced manifold, advances under a vector field conjugate to the semantic dynamics, and answers as a function of manifold coordinates that respond to interventions.
Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item A geometric definition of $\varepsilon$-SMIR with tubular support, flow conjugacy, manifold sufficiency, and intervention--tangent equivalence, plus a curvature-sensitive composition bound.
\item \textbf{Certificate decomposition} (Theorem~\ref{thm:decomp}): certifiability $\Leftrightarrow$ legal chart $E$ $\wedge$ conjugate dynamics $V$ $\wedge$ faithful readout $Q$, with distinct \textbf{Oracle-F} and \textbf{Flow-F} metrics.
\item \textbf{Wrong-chart impossibility} (Theorem~\ref{thm:wrong}): charts that collapse intervention-distinguishable states cannot be certified by any Lipschitz readout.
\item A \textbf{gated learnability} protocol (Proposition~\ref{prop:gate}): train $Q$ on the true field first, then learn routers; joint annealing without a gate has no guarantee.
\item Controlled experiments supporting the Hard upper bound, gated Discrete/Soft routers, text-chart failure, and conjugacy ablations.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Scope (what we do \emph{not} claim).}
We study a shadow domain with known semantic states.
We do not claim unrestricted natural-language certificates without a legal chart.
Hard structured $V$ yields $\eta\!\approx\!0$ \emph{by construction}; it certifies the readout leg, not that conjugacy was learned from scratch.
Riemannian comparison, tubular neighborhoods, and intervention tests are classical tools; our novelty is the packaged certificate for latent reasoning with separable falsification.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent and continuous reasoning.}
Coconut-style methods replace explicit CoT tokens by continuous states updated inside the model~\citep{hao2024coconut}.
Follow-ups study continuous distillation and latent bridges~\citep{zhu2025codi,latentcot2025mech}.
Mechanistic evidence suggests that longer latent budgets need not implement full step-by-step rollouts and may rely on late fusion~\citep{latentcot2025mech}.
Faithfulness measured by input counterfactuals and noise ablation can change non-monotonically across checkpoints~\citep{faithful2025latent}.
SMIR asks a complementary question: when is a latent flow geometrically conjugate to an explicit semantic dynamical system?

\paragraph{Causal abstraction and representation alignment.}
Causal abstraction formalizes when a high-level algorithm is a faithful simplification of a network~\citep{geiger2021causal}.
DAS learns distributed alignments for interchange interventions~\citep{geiger2024das}.
If alignment maps are unrestricted and nonlinear, abstraction can become vacuous~\citep{nonlinear2025}.
SMIR takes a different cut: fix or test a chart, then certify dynamics conjugacy and readout faithfulness, and prove that some charts are impossible to certify.

\paragraph{On-manifold interventions.}
Activation patching off the data manifold can activate spurious pathways~\citep{onmanifold2025}.
Tubular neighborhoods provide local normal-bundle coordinates for controlled interventions.
We use tubular support as one leg of a reasoning certificate, tied to semantic metrics rather than activation density alone.

\paragraph{Faithfulness of verbalized reasoning.}
A large literature shows that natural-language CoT need not cause model predictions~\citep{turpin2023faithfulness,lanham2023measuring}.
Our Oracle-F / Flow-F metrics adapt intervention tests to chart-encoded states versus learned latent rollouts, targeting latent rather than verbal traces.

\section{SMIR Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

This section develops the formal certificate.
We first define the semantic geometry, then the four geometric conditions, then the main theorems used in the experiments (decomposition, wrong charts, gated learnability), and finally the variational objective.

\subsection{Semantic manifold and intervention metric}
Let $\cD$ be a task family and $\mathcal{S}$ the set of legitimate intermediate reasoning states, quotiented by future behavioral equivalence (states that induce the same answer law under all continuations are identified).
\begin{definition}[Semantic manifold]
\label{def:manifold}
We assume there exists a smooth $d$-dimensional manifold $\cM$ with $d\ll D$ and an embedding $\iota:\cM\hookrightarrow\mathbb{R}^n$ identifying the continuous relaxation of $\mathcal{S}$.
\end{definition}
Intuitively, register configurations, proof stacks, or graph-search frontiers have far fewer degrees of freedom than ambient neural activations.

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a compact family of continuations/queries and $K:\cM\times\mathcal{C}\to\Delta(\mathcal{Y})$ a $C^2$ behavioral kernel ($|\mathcal{Y}|<\infty$).
\begin{definition}[Intervention metric]
\label{def:gstar}
In a local chart, for $v\in T_s\cM$,
\begin{equation}
g^*_s(v,v)
\;:=\;
\sup_{c\in\mathcal{C}}
\Big\|
\partial_\varepsilon\big|_{0}
K(s+\varepsilon v,\,c)
\Big\|_{\TV}^2.
\label{eq:gstar}
\end{equation}
A Fisher--Rao style variant replaces the supremum by an expectation of score inner products.
On the open set where $g^*$ is positive definite, $(\cM,g^*)$ is Riemannian.
\end{definition}
Geodesics of $g^*$ are the ``cheapest'' state changes in an intervention sense: directions invisible to all continuations have zero length.

\subsection{Semantic dynamics and latent models}
Legal operators $a\in\mathcal{A}$ induce maps $f_a:\cM\to\cM$.
Writing $\phi_t^{a}$ for a continuous-time expansion (or using $\log$-maps for discrete steps),
\begin{equation}
V^*_a(s)
\;:=\;
\frac{d}{dt}\Big|_{0}\phi_t^{a}(s)
\in T_s\cM,
\qquad
\dot s = V^*(s,u(t)),\quad u(t)\in\mathcal{U}.
\label{eq:semflow}
\end{equation}
A neural model exposes ambient latents $z\in\mathbb{R}^D$, a chart $E_\theta:\cM\to\mathbb{R}^D$, a controlled field $V_\theta(z,u)$, and a readout $Q_\theta$:
\begin{equation}
\dot z = V_\theta(z,u),
\qquad
\hat y \sim Q_\theta(z).
\label{eq:latflow}
\end{equation}
Discrete Coconut-style updates are Euler steps of~\eqref{eq:latflow}; SMIR requires them to track~\eqref{eq:semflow} under $E_\theta$.

\paragraph{Standing assumptions (used below).}
$E_\theta$ is a $C^2$ immersion and locally bi-Lipschitz onto its image; $V_\theta$ is $C^1$ and $L$-Lipschitz on a tubular neighborhood of radius $\varepsilon_0$; $Q_\theta$ is $\Lambda$-Lipschitz in total variation; sectional curvature on the working region is bounded by $\kappa$ and the injectivity radius is bounded below.

\subsection{Four geometric conditions}
\begin{definition}[$\varepsilon$-SMIR]
\label{def:smir}
$\theta$ implements $\varepsilon$-SMIR on $\cD$ with budget $T$ and intervention family $\mathcal{I}$ if, with probability at least $1-\delta$ along trajectories,
\begin{align}
&\textbf{(G1) Tubular support:}\quad
z(t)\in
\Tub_\varepsilon\!\big(E_\theta(\cM)\big)
:=\big\{z:\mathrm{dist}\big(z,E_\theta(\cM)\big)\le\varepsilon\big\};
\label{eq:g1}\\
&\textbf{(G2) Flow conjugacy:}\quad
\big\|
DE_\theta(s)[V^*(s,u)]-V_\theta(E_\theta(s),u)
\big\|
\le\eta;
\label{eq:g2}\\
&\textbf{(G3) Manifold sufficiency:}\quad
\exists\,q,\Pi\text{ with }
\TV\big(Q_\theta(z),q(\Pi(z))\big)\le\varepsilon_Q;
\label{eq:g3}\\
&\textbf{(G4) Intervention--tangent equivalence:}\quad
\nonumber\\
&\qquad
do\!\big(s\leftarrow\exp_s(v)\big)
\;\longleftrightarrow\;
z\leftarrow E_\theta(s)+DE_\theta[v]+O(\|v\|^2),
\nonumber\\
&\qquad\text{with TV response matching $\sqrt{g^*_s(v,v)}$ up to $\varepsilon_I$.}
\label{eq:g4}
\end{align}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Relation to sufficiency / faithfulness axioms.}
G1+G3 operationalize sufficiency (answers ignore normal/bypass coordinates).
G2+G4 operationalize intervention faithfulness along trajectories.
Integrating G2 in time yields composition closure (next theorem).
Thus classical S/F-style criteria appear as coordinate projections of SMIR, with explicit curvature and tube-radius error terms.

\subsection{Off-manifold shortcuts are falsifiable}
\begin{theorem}[Off-manifold mass implies fake reasoning]
\label{thm:off}
Suppose $Q_\theta$ is Lipschitz and there exists a shortcut set $\mathcal{Z}_{\mathrm{short}}$ with $\mathrm{dist}(\mathcal{Z}_{\mathrm{short}},E_\theta(\cM))\ge\varepsilon>0$ that attains high accuracy under the non-interventional data distribution.
If $g^*$ separates some pair via $\mathcal{I}$, then there exists an intervention battery with probability at least $p_0(\varepsilon,\mathcal{I})>0$ on which manifold trajectories change answers as semantics require, while shortcut trajectories change by $O(e^{-\kappa\varepsilon})$ (or remain invariant) whenever normal derivatives of $Q$ are suppressed by G3.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof idea]
Use Fermi coordinates $(x,n)$ along the chart image.
G3 forces $\partial_n Q$ small for $\|n\|\ge\varepsilon$, so tangential semantic interventions barely move shortcut predictions, while $g^*$ lower-bounds the on-manifold response.
\end{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:off} is the geometric teeth behind ``accuracy $\neq$ reasoning'': high accuracy off the tube is \emph{refutable} by interventions.

\subsection{Conjugacy composition bound}
Write $e_k=\|z_k-E_\theta(s_k)\|$ for discrete steps of size $\Delta t$ with $N\Delta t=T$.
\begin{theorem}[Composition bound]
\label{thm:compose}
Under the standing assumptions and one-step conjugacy $\le\eta$,
\begin{equation}
e_{k+1}
\;\le\;
(1+L\Delta t)\,e_k
+\eta\Delta t
+C(\kappa)\,\mathrm{inj}^{-1}e_k^2\Delta t
+O((\Delta t)^2).
\label{eq:compose}
\end{equation}
Iterating and linearizing the leading terms yields
\begin{equation}
e_N
\;\le\;
e^{LT}e_0
+\eta\frac{e^{LT}-1}{L}
+O\!\big(\kappa\cdot\mathrm{poly}(e^{LT})\cdot\sup_k e_k\big).
\label{eq:composeN}
\end{equation}
If the readout has TV sensitivity $\le\Lambda$, terminal answer error satisfies
$\TV(\hat Y,Y^*)\le\Lambda e_N+\varepsilon_Q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof idea]
Split the error into tangential conjugacy residual and normal drift in the tubular neighborhood; apply discrete Gronwall to the linear part and Riemannian comparison for the quadratic curvature remainder.
\end{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:composeN} is why multi-step latent CoT cannot be certified from endpoint accuracy alone: errors accumulate as $e^{LT}$ unless $\eta$ and the tube radius are controlled.

\subsection{Pullback metric matching}
Let $\tilde g = E_\theta^* g_{\mathrm{eucl}}$ (or a model-induced ambient metric).
\begin{theorem}[Local intervention faithfulness from metric match]
\label{thm:metric}
If $\|\tilde g-g^*\|_{C^0(U)}\le\varepsilon_g$ on a chart neighborhood $U$, then for sufficiently small tangential $v$,
\begin{equation}
\Big|
\mathrm{TV\textrm{-}response}\big(E_\theta(s);v\big)
-\sqrt{g^*_s(v,v)}
\Big|
\;\le\;
O(\varepsilon_g\|v\|+\|v\|^2).
\label{eq:metric}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
Thus G4 is not an extra heuristic: it follows from aligning the pullback geometry with $g^*$.
When $E$ is a known scaled identity chart and $Q$ is calibrated in those coordinates, matching $g^*$ reduces largely to enforcing Oracle-F on distinguishing batteries (used in our experiments).

\subsection{Certificate decomposition}
On a shadow domain we operationalize
\begin{align}
\varepsilon_E
&=\EE\big[\mathrm{dist}(z_t^{\mathrm{obs}},E(s_t))\big],
\label{eq:epsE}\\
\eta_V
&=\EE\big\|V_\theta(E(s),u)-DE[V^*(s,u)]\big\|,
\label{eq:etaV}\\
\delta_Q
&=\EE\big[\indicator\{\arg\max Q(E(s^+))\neq \arg\max Q(E(s^-))\}\big],
\label{eq:oracleF}
\end{align}
and Flow-F $\delta_Q^{\mathrm{flow}}$ by comparing the learned rollout from $s_0$ against the wrong-branch terminal (same query).

\begin{theorem}[Decomposition]
\label{thm:decomp}
Under the standing assumptions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Necessity.}
$\varepsilon$-SMIR implies $\varepsilon_E=O(\varepsilon)$, $\eta_V\le\eta$, and a positive lower bound on $\delta_Q$ and $\delta_Q^{\mathrm{flow}}$ for intervention-rich batteries.
\item \textbf{Sufficiency.}
If $\varepsilon_E$ and $\eta_V$ are small and Oracle-F is high on $E(\cM)$, then
\begin{equation}
\delta_Q^{\mathrm{flow}}
\;\ge\;
\delta_Q
- C\Big(\varepsilon_E+\eta\tfrac{e^{LT}-1}{L}+\varepsilon_Q\Big).
\label{eq:flowf}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Separability.}
Fixing two blocks and varying one yields leg-specific falsification:
freeze $(V^*,E)$ to test $Q$; freeze $(Q,E)$ to test $V$; replace $E$ by a wrong chart to test Theorem~\ref{thm:wrong}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Necessity projects G1--G4 onto~\eqref{eq:epsE}--\eqref{eq:oracleF} and applies Theorem~\ref{thm:compose} for Flow-F.
For sufficiency, bound $\|z_T-E(s_T)\|$ by~\eqref{eq:composeN}; Lipschitzness of $Q$ limits hard-decision flips between Oracle and Flow batteries, giving~\eqref{eq:flowf}.
Separability is immediate from the dependence graph of the three errors.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Hard-$V$ vs.\ learnable $V$]
\label{rem:hard}
Success with frozen true dynamics and trained $Q$ establishes only the readout leg.
It does \emph{not} imply that arbitrary $V_\theta$ is learnable; Soft/MLP underperformance remains compatible with Hard success.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Wrong charts and chart equivalence}
\begin{definition}[$\gamma$-wrong chart]
\label{def:wrong}
$E^\dagger$ is a $\gamma$-wrong chart if there exists an intervention-distinguishable pair with semantic response $\ge\gamma$ yet $\|E^\dagger(s^+)-E^\dagger(s^-)\|\le\tau$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Wrong charts are uncertifiable]
\label{thm:wrong}
If $\tau<\gamma/(2L_Q)$, then for every $L_Q$-Lipschitz readout depending only on the image of $E^\dagger$,
\[
\TV\big(Q(E^\dagger(s^+)),Q(E^\dagger(s^-))\big)
\le L_Q\tau
<\gamma/2,
\]
so G4 fails on that battery.
If the support of true trajectories is essentially disjoint from $\Tub_\varepsilon(E^\dagger(\cM))$, G1 fails as well.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Last-token interfaces]
\label{cor:text}
If a text encoder induces a $\gamma$-wrong chart relative to the semantic quotient (folding or scale pathology), then under a shared-label shadow protocol one expects F$\approx 0$ and tubular blow-up---as structural consequences, not tuning accidents.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Chart equivalence]
\label{thm:equiv}
If $\Psi$ is a fiber-preserving $C^1$ bi-Lipschitz map on a tube with $E_2=\Psi\circ E_1$, $V_2=(D\Psi)V_1\circ\Psi^{-1}$, and $Q_2=Q_1\circ\Psi^{-1}$, then $(E_1,V_1,Q_1)$ and $(E_2,V_2,Q_2)$ succeed or fail Definition~\ref{def:smir} together (up to constant rescaling).
In particular, affine rescalings $E\mapsto aE+b$ ($a\neq 0$) are certificate-equivalent, while irreversible projections with nontrivial kernel intersecting $T\cM$ along $g^*$-separable directions are generally not.
\end{theorem}
Theorem~\ref{thm:equiv} justifies using $E(s)=s/c$ as a legal representative without claiming that the chart was learned from data.

\subsection{Gated learnability}
Joint optimization of $(V,Q)$ is nonconvex.
We do not claim global convergence of gradient descent; instead we give a checkable sufficient protocol.

\begin{proposition}[Gated learnability]
\label{prop:gate}
Fix a legal chart.
If stage~A trains $Q$ on the true field $V^*$ to Oracle-F $\ge 1-\alpha$ and bounded task risk, then any subsequent stage that reduces $\eta_V$ to $\eta$ while keeping $Q$ $\varepsilon_Q$-stable on $E(\cM)$ satisfies~\eqref{eq:flowf}.
Conversely, if stage~A only achieves Oracle-F $\le\beta\ll 1$, then~\eqref{eq:flowf} cannot exceed $\beta$ up to $o(1)$, regardless of later conjugacy improvements; early annealing that inflates $\eta_V$ further suppresses Flow-F.
\end{proposition}
Proposition~\ref{prop:gate} is the theoretical justification for Hard$\to Q$ transfer before learning Discrete/Soft routers, and for treating no-gate joint Soft as a negative control.

\subsection{Variational principle and minimal objectives}
Matching semantic and latent controlled actions yields a Dirichlet-type correspondence.
\begin{theorem}[Action matching; informal]
\label{thm:var}
If $z=E_\theta\circ s$ and both conjugacy~\eqref{eq:g2} and metric match~\eqref{eq:metric} hold, then the latent action functional and the semantic action functional differ by at most $C_T(\eta+\varepsilon_g)$.
Conversely, uniformly small latent action on a compact trajectory class forces small integrated conjugacy residuals.
\end{theorem}
Expanding the geometry into training terms gives
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SMIR}}
&=
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}
+\lambda_1\EE\|V_\theta(E(s),u)-DE[V^*]\|^2
\\
&\quad
+\lambda_2\EE\|\tilde g_s-g^*_s\|_F^2
+\lambda_3\EE\big[\mathrm{dist}^2(z_t,E(\cM))\big]
\\
&\quad
+\lambda_4\EE\big[\TV(Q(z),Q(E(\Pi(z))))\big].
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:lfull}
\end{equation}
When $E$ is fixed and legal (Theorem~\ref{thm:equiv}), tubular and metric penalties become largely redundant with conjugacy plus Oracle-F, yielding the minimal objective used in our gated runs:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\min}
=
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}
+\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{dist}}
+\lambda_V\|V_\theta(E(s),u)-DE[V^*]\|^2.
\label{eq:lmin}
\end{equation}
Learning $E$ itself requires restoring $\lambda_2,\lambda_3$; otherwise Theorem~\ref{thm:wrong} applies.

\subsection{Discrete CoT as a polygonal chart path}
\begin{proposition}[CoT polygons]
\label{prop:cot}
Pointwise distillation that matches $E(s^{(i)})\approx z_{t_i}$ at annotated CoT states does \emph{not} imply small $\eta$.
If additionally $\eta$ is small and endpoints agree, sufficiently fine CoT polygons are Hausdorff-close to the continuous semantic trajectory in $g^*$.
\end{proposition}
This explains ``distillation succeeded but interventions failed'': point matching without conjugacy leaves freedom for off-manifold shortcuts between annotated knots.

\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Shadow domain}
\textbf{SMIR-Shadow-Long} generates 5-register programs with operations $\{\mathrm{add},\mathrm{sub},\mathrm{mul}\}$.
Training chains have lengths in $\{2,3,4,5\}$; OOD evaluation emphasizes longer chains.
Each example includes the full state trajectory $\{s_t\}$, actions, a continuation query, and---when distinguishing---a wrong terminal state that flips the label under the same query.
This yields an explicit semantic field $V^*$ and intervention batteries for Oracle-F / Flow-F.

\subsection{Architectures}
All methods share a legal chart $E(s)=s/c$ ($c{=}40$) unless stated otherwise, a query encoder, and a binary answer head $Q$.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Hard-$V$}: exact structured register transitions; train only $Q$ (readout-leg upper bound / M1).
\item \textbf{Discrete-gated}: transfer $Q$ from Hard; learn a discrete op router executed with hard arithmetic (Gumbel-softmax STE).
\item \textbf{Soft-gated}: transfer $Q$; anneal soft mixture bias $5\!\to\!0$ only after Oracle-F gate.
\item \textbf{Soft-joint}: Soft router trained jointly without the Hard$\to Q$ gate (negative control).
\item \textbf{MLP$\pm$teacher}: unstructured residual $V$ with / without absolute teacher forcing (ablation).
\item \textbf{Text chart}: frozen 7B last-token embeddings as $E^\dagger$ with a probe head (Theorem~\ref{thm:wrong} instance).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Training and gates}
Hard curricula follow a short-chain high-distinguishing phase then mixed long chains (optional F-recovery).
Gated routers freeze or lightly fine-tune $Q$ while optimizing routing with conjugacy / route CE terms (Eq.~\ref{eq:lmin}).
\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Gated learnability (Prop.~\ref{prop:gate})}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State Train $Q$ on Hard $V^*$ until Oracle-F $\ge \tau_{\mathrm{gate}}$ and OOD is healthy
\State Transfer $Q$ into Discrete or Soft architecture
\If{Oracle-F still $\ge \tau_{\mathrm{gate}}$}
  \State Learn router $V$ (optionally anneal Soft bias); monitor Flow-F
\Else
  \State Abort anneal; report gate failure
\EndIf
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Success thresholds.}
\textbf{M1 (Hard):} Oracle-F$\ge 0.5$, OOD$\ge 0.8$, $\eta<0.02$, tub$<0.05$, sufficiency gap $\ge 0.15$.
\textbf{M2 (learnable):} Flow-F$\ge 0.45$ with relaxed $\eta$/tub tolerances appropriate for approximate routers.

Table~\ref{tab:ablate} shows that minimizing task loss alone does not yield SMIR certificates.
Teacher forcing reduces conjugacy error relative to task-only MLP but still underperforms gated structured routers on Oracle-F and OOD.

\subsection{Length behavior}
For Soft-gated, task accuracy across evaluation horizons $\{4,5,6,7,9\}$ stays in $[0.80,0.84]$, consistent with a controlled composition error under small $\eta$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:compose}).
Hard and Discrete show similarly flat length curves in the same range, whereas text-chart OOD remains weak.

\subsection{What the metrics buy}
Oracle-F diagnoses $Q\circ E$ on true states; Flow-F diagnoses whether the learned $V$ preserves distinguishing structure under rollout.
Reporting only task accuracy---or only Oracle-F after freezing Hard $V$---would overstate latent reasoning.
We recommend both F metrics whenever latent CoT is claimed.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discuss}

\paragraph{Positioning.}
SMIR's incremental contribution is the certificate \emph{package}: decomposition, wrong-chart impossibility, and gated learnability for latent reasoning---not the invention of manifolds or interventions per se.
This clarifies how Hard upper bounds, learnable routers, and LLM text-chart negatives can be true simultaneously without contradiction.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Experiments focus on a controlled register domain; multi-seed aggregates and a second semantic domain (e.g., graph reachability shadows) are natural extensions.
Our LLM evidence is a negative chart result, not a positive large-scale latent CoT system.
Open theory includes chart identifiability without shadows, Transformer--flow consistency, and global convergence without gates.

\paragraph{Broader impact.}
Certificates can reduce overclaiming about ``latent reasoning,'' but shadow supervision may be unavailable in the wild; Theorem~\ref{thm:wrong} still warns against treating arbitrary embeddings as legal charts.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

We proposed SMIR, a geometric certificate for implicit reasoning based on tubular support, flow conjugacy, and intervention-faithful readouts.
Certificate decomposition and wrong-chart impossibility specify what latent CoT evaluations can and cannot claim.
Controlled experiments support a Hard readout upper bound, gated Discrete/Soft routers with strong Flow-F, and text-chart failure.
We advocate reporting Oracle-F and Flow-F alongside accuracy whenever latent reasoning is claimed.

\section{Additional formal details}
\label{app:formal}
\paragraph{Metric.}
One workable form is
$g^*_s(v,v)=\sup_c \|\partial_\varepsilon|_0 K(s+\varepsilon v,c)\|_{\TV}^2$,
with Fisher variants when kernels are smooth.
\paragraph{Decomposition proof idea.}
Necessity projects G1--G4 onto $(\varepsilon_E,\eta_V,\delta_Q)$.
Sufficiency applies Theorem~\ref{thm:compose} to bound $\|z_T-E(s_T)\|$, then uses readout Lipschitzness to control hard-decision flips between Oracle and Flow batteries, yielding~\eqref{eq:flowf}.
\paragraph{Wrong-chart proof idea.}
Lipschitz $Q$ cannot create TV response larger than $L_Q\tau$; if this is $<\gamma/2$ while semantics respond by $\ge\gamma$, G4 fails.
